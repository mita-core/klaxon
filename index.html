About

Est. 2019 · Brooklyn, NY

We roast six coffees on a 30 lb drum in East Williamsburg. Not forty. Six is enough to cover espresso, filter, decaf and cold brew, and few enough that every batch leaves the roastery within two days.

We buy through two importers who publish what they pay farmers. We print the roast date, the lot and the price on the bag. Nothing else needs saying — the coffee is loud enough on its own.
